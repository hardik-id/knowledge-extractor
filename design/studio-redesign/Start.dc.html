Leave rules 2027

Source: Confluence · HR / Policies · last modified 20 November 2026

These rules replace the leave rules of 2026. Questions go to the HR team.

Rule 1: Requesting leave

Rule: Leave must be requested in the self-service portal before it starts.
Kind: obligation
Applies to: all permanent staff, temporary staff
Condition: The leave lasts more than half a day.
Effective from: 1 January 2027

Exceptions

  • Sick leave, which is reported by phone on the first day

Rule 2: Working from abroad

Rule: Employees may work from another country for up to 20 days a year.
Kind: permission
Applies to: all permanent staff
Condition: The manager agrees and the country is on the approved list.
Effective from: 1 March 2027
